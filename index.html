02 // Scope

What this notice covers

This notice covers the kryptik.io pre-launch waitlist landing page at kryptik.io, including:

  • visiting and browsing the waitlist page hosted through GitHub Pages;
  • submitting an email address and consent response through the waitlist form; and
  • loading the public market-data ticker, which requests public data from CoinPaprika and Binance services; and clicking a ticker item to open a market chart or search on Binance or DEX Screener.

The waitlist is intended for adults aged 18 or over. Do not submit personal data if you are under 18.

This notice does not cover any separate kryptik.io trading website, terminal, funded account, identity or eligibility checks, payment processing, trading activity, challenge monitoring or customer support systems. We will host those services separately and provide separate privacy information before collecting personal data through them.

03 // Data categories

What information do we use?

Information you provide

  • Email address - the address you enter in the waitlist form.
  • Consent response - whether you tick the required consent checkbox and the consent wording associated with your submission.
  • Submission record - Google Forms records the response and ordinarily provides a response timestamp in the linked response spreadsheet.

Technical information

When you load this page, GitHub Pages may log your IP address for security purposes, together with technical request information. When the browser requests public ticker data, CoinPaprika and Binance may receive your IP address, request details, browser/device information and the time of the request. When you click a ticker item, the chart destination (Binance for a matched spot pair or DEX Screener for a search result) may receive your IP address and ordinary browser request information. When you submit the Google Form, Google receives the form fields and technical information associated with the request.

Information we do not request

The waitlist form does not ask for your name, date of birth, identity documents, wallet address, payment details or trading history. Please do not put sensitive personal data or information about another person into the email field or any communication with us unless it is necessary for a specific request.

We use Google Forms to collect waitlist submissions and Google Sheets to organise the responses. We access these services through a personal Google Account. When you submit the form, Google receives the information you provide and related technical information. Google's processing of information through these services is governed by the terms and privacy information applicable to the account and services used. For further information about Google's data practices, please consult Google's Privacy Policy and the terms applicable to Google Forms and Google Sheets.

We restrict access to the form and response spreadsheet to the account owner and individuals specifically authorised by us who need access to administer the waitlist.

Submitting the form is voluntary. If you do not provide an email address and select the consent checkbox, we cannot register you for waitlist communications. Joining the waitlist does not create a trading account, guarantee admission, confer a right to funding or constitute a contract for a financial or crypto-asset service.
